Case File No. 17 — The Study

The Vanishing

Thursday — 11:47 p.m.

The suspect vanished into thin air… but every room hides a secret perspective.

  • Rust-brown stains: one on a crate, one atop a column, one on a stack of books. Scattered. Meaningless.
  • The butler swears the front door stayed bolted all night.
  • Lord Blackwood's study has one way in. Supposedly.

A good detective doesn't move the evidence. She moves herself.

There.

11:52 p.m.

Standing just so, the stains stop being stains. They're footsteps.

One track, heel to toe, across the rug and away from the door. Follow it to where it ends — the last print, glowing by the east wall.
